How much does it cost to build a mobile app? +

Cost depends on features, integrations, platform choice, design complexity and launch requirements. After discovery, we provide a realistic scope and estimate.

Should I build iOS, Android or cross-platform? +

We recommend the platform based on your users, roadmap, budget and launch priorities. Cross-platform frameworks can be effective when speed and shared code matter.

Do you sign an NDA before discussing my idea? +

Yes. An NDA can be arranged before detailed project discussion or requirements review.
